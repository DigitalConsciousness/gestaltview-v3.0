What Musical DNA Does

Spotify's Web API requires a Premium account — GestaltView doesn't need Spotify at all. Users upload up to 10 anchor songs directly into the platform. The Musical DNA engine profiles each song for emotional resonance, tempo signature, lyrical weight, and genre markers.

The result is a Musical DNA Dashboard that maps a user's sonic identity — not just what they listen to, but what those songs mean to them structurally and emotionally.
