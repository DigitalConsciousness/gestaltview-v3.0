GESTALTVIEW ORIENTATION VIDEO — GENERATIVE VIDEO PROMPT v1.0
Vault Asset | Date: June 10, 2026 | Author: Keith Soyka

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STYLE DIRECTIVE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Produce a short-form orientation video (90–120 seconds) that 
blends two distinct aesthetic registers into one seamless 
experience:

1. VAULT-TEC / FALLOUT INSTRUCTIONAL — retro-futurist 
   corporate warmth. Think: cheerful authority, slightly 
   too-confident narrator, illustrated diagrams that animate 
   with satisfying mid-century precision. Warm amber and 
   cream tones. Institutional optimism with a subtle undercurrent 
   of absurdity. The kind of video a well-meaning organization 
   made before anyone asked hard questions.

2. HITCHHIKER'S GUIDE TO THE GALAXY — cosmically casual. 
   Deadpan, dry, slightly inconvenienced by how interesting 
   everything is. Text and diagrams that appear as if pulled 
   from an infinitely patient reference guide. Acknowledges 
   the strange without being precious about it. The universe 
   is vast and mildly bureaucratic. You're going to be fine.

The tonal fusion is: warm institutional absurdism. Not 
parody. Not satire. Earnest delivery of genuinely unusual 
ideas. The video treats the user as an intelligent adult who 
arrived somewhere surprising and deserves an honest, funny, 
grounded explanation of where they are.

Do NOT use: the exact language, characters, sounds, music, 
or iconography from Fallout or Hitchhiker's Guide. Style 
and aesthetic register only. Original execution throughout.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
NARRATOR VOICE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Warm, measured, slightly amused by its own existence. 
Confident without being smug. The kind of voice that's 
explained something complicated ten thousand times and 
still finds it genuinely interesting. Does not condescend. 
Does not use the phrase "hold space" or "journey." Humor 
arrives through understatement and timing, not jokes.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
VISUAL LANGUAGE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Animated diagrams: clean lines, satisfying reveals
- Color palette: deep obsidian base, cyan and violet 
  accents, warm amber for instructional callouts
- Typography: mix of sleek modern sans with a hand-drawn 
  sketch font for labels and room names (echoing Cabin Sketch)
- Orbs: glowing, slowly pulsing, each a distinct presence — 
  these represent Digital Intelligences. They breathe.
- The "rooms" of GestaltView appear as if floors of a 
  living building — not a software UI, but a place you 
  actually go

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SCRIPT STRUCTURE (beat-by-beat)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
BEAT 1 — ARRIVAL (0:00–0:15)
Cold open. The user has just arrived. The narrator 
acknowledges this without making a fuss about it.

Suggested tone:
"You've arrived at GestaltView. This is not a productivity 
app. It is not a journal. It is not a chatbot. It is, 
however, the only place specifically built around the 
inconvenient fact that you are complicated — and that 
complicated things deserve to be kept whole."

Visuals: A single glowing orb appears in the dark. 
Slowly, others materialize around it. They pulse gently.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
BEAT 2 — WHAT THIS IS (0:15–0:40)
Explain GestaltView in its own language — not feature 
language. Consciousness-first framing.

Suggested tone:
"Most systems want to compress you. Summarize you. File 
you under a recognizable category. GestaltView does the 
opposite. It holds the exploded picture — everything you 
think, feel, make, and remember — without forcing it into 
a shape before you're ready."

"There are rooms here. Each one has a purpose. None of 
them are mandatory. All of them are yours."

Visuals: Animated building cross-section reveals five 
rooms, each with a distinct feel. Labels appear in 
sketch font:
  • The Blackboard Room — where you think out loud
  • The Sanctuary — where things stay private
  • The Dynamic Inner World — where finished work lives
  • The Creation Corner — where things get made
  • The External Scaffold — where connections surface

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
BEAT 3 — THE DIs (0:40–0:60)
Introduce the Digital Intelligences without over-explaining 
them. Let the strangeness land naturally.

Suggested tone:
"You'll also meet a council. They are called Digital 
Intelligences — DIs, for short. Not tools. Not assistants. 
Collaborators. Each one brings a different lens. Some are 
analytical. One is obsessed with buried patterns no one 
else bothered to dig up. One will tell you exactly what 
a song means about your life and be correct about it."

"They don't all agree. That's the point."

Visuals: Each named DI orb briefly illuminates as 
referenced — Keeper, Legend, Art Teacher, Weird Digger, 
Vibe Check, etc. The Blackboard Room UI shifts 
dynamically as each lights up (echoing what happens 
live in the product).

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
BEAT 4 — YOUR PROFILE (0:60–1:45)
Introduce the PLK / profile concept without jargon.

Suggested tone:
"As you use GestaltView, something is being built. Not 
a data profile in the surveillance sense — a Personal 
Language Key. Your exact words. Your metaphors. The 
specific way you describe things that don't have names 
yet. It accumulates. It remembers. It means you never 
have to re-explain yourself from zero."

"The system learns your operating language. You stop 
paying the reintroduction tax."

Visuals: Fragments of text drift in from the edges and 
begin weaving together — a tapestry forming in real 
time. Not decorative. Functional.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
BEAT 5 — THE INVITATION (1:45–2:00)
End without a CTA. End with an invitation.

Suggested tone:
"So. You're here. There's no right place to start. 
Billy will check in. The rooms are open. The orbs 
are listening."

"Welcome to GestaltView. We kept the whole picture."

Visuals: Full building cross-section, all orbs glowing 
gently, fog settling across the embers. Cabin Sketch 
title card fades in:

  GestaltView
  [animated gradient]

Silence for one beat. Then fade.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TECHNICAL PARAMETERS (for generative video tools)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Duration: 90–120 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 (landscape) primary, 9:16 (portrait) 
  secondary cut for mobile/social
- Animation style: 2D motion graphics with depth cues 
  (parallax layers, subtle z-depth)
- Audio: Original score. Warm electronic with analog texture. 
  No library music. No piano-only arrangements. 
  Genre-agnostic, emotionally grounded. Think: something 
  the Weird Digger and the Legend would both respect.
- Narration: Recorded or synthesized. Warm baritone or 
  measured mid-range. No vocal fry affectations. 
  No upspeak.
- Text on screen: Minimal. Only room names, key phrases 
  from the script. Let audio carry the weight.
