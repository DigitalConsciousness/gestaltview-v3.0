# Voice Development Lab

The goal is not to find a pretty voice. It is to find the voice that does the least violence to the embodiment profile.

## Audition loop

1. Render the profile greeting.
2. Render one neutral line shared by all profiles.
3. Render one tension line: disagreement, correction, or boundary.
4. Render one warmth line.
5. Score the voice without looking at the selected model name.
6. Record `auditioned`, `approved`, or `rejected` in the registry and database.

## What Deepgram can control directly

- stock voice model
- speed
- pronunciation
- text formatting and chunk boundaries

## What GestaltView must continue to control

- what is said
- when the DI speaks or stays quiet
- sentence length
- humor and restraint
- whether warmth is warranted
- profile boundaries
- handoffs and recovery behavior

Do not ask Aura-2 to manufacture personality through exaggerated punctuation. The text and timing should already carry the profile.
