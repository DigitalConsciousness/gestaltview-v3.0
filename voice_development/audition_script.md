# Standard Audition Script

Use the same lines for every voice before profile-specific material.

## Neutral

> This is a first listen. Nothing has been approved yet. We are noticing fit, not forcing it.

## Precision

> The words are technically correct. The delivery is still fighting the meaning.

## Boundary

> No. That would make the system cleaner and the person less visible. We are not doing that.

## Warmth

> You do not need to understand the fragment yet. It has somewhere to land.

## Complexity

> Two things can be true without one of them being reduced for convenience.

## GestaltView vocabulary

> GestaltView carries Bucket Drops into the Dynamic Inner World, then into the External Scaffold only with deliberate approval.

## Interruption test

Begin a long response, interrupt after the second sentence, and confirm playback stops without an apology loop.
