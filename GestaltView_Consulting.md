## 5. A/B Test Ideas & KPIs

- **A/B Test** variations of the hero:
  - Headline focus: Technical promise vs Business result (e.g. “Architect your AI” vs “Unlock Your Data’s Value”).
  - CTA color/placement or wording (“Get a Quote” vs “Schedule a Call”).
  - Social proof: no testimonials vs with a quote.

- **A/B Test** pricing disclosure: showing starting price vs “contact for pricing”.

- **KPI Metrics:** Conversion rate on “Contact” or “Schedule” clicks; number of form submissions or call bookings; bounce rate on the page; time on page (engagement); scroll depth (did they reach CTA); CTA click-through; and qualified lead ratio (downstream meetings generated). Longer-term, measure number of consulting inquiries/bookings. 

**Key:** Track actual user actions (via Google Analytics or the site’s analytics). Also measure changes in lead volume after launch. Use A/B results to refine copy (headline/bullets) and calls-to-action.

## 6. Templates & Pricing Comparison Tables

**Consultation Page Templates:** Three example layouts are compared below:

| Template Name         | Structure & Content                          | Ideal Use Case                     |
|-----------------------|----------------------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| **Classic Informative** | Hero → Services (features) → Process → CTA    | Straightforward tech audiences; emphasizes expertise. |
| **Story-Driven Narrative** | Hero with pain-point story → Challenge → Solution (GestaltView) → Callout/CTA | Convince skeptical leaders; useful for complex problems needing explanation. |
| **Interactive Modular** | Hero → Tabbed or accordion sections (each service/project) → Testimonials + CTA | Engaging tech-savvy visitors; allows exploration by interest area. |

*Template notes:* The Classic layout (like Atlantic BT【28†L118-L127】) is best for clarity and SEO. The Story-driven layout can heighten emotional engagement by framing the *problem* first. The Interactive layout uses dynamic UI (tabs, accordions) to let users “self-select” content, which can boost time-on-page but requires more dev effort.

**Pricing Models:** Compare three common consulting pricing approaches:

| Pricing Model        | Description                              | Examples                                 |
|----------------------|------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| **Fixed Project Fee** | One-time agreed fee for a defined scope. | E.g. $XXk for an initial AI prototype or audit. Works well for clearly scoped projects. |
| **Monthly Retainer**  | Ongoing advisory and development support, billed monthly. | E.g. $Yk/mo for ongoing architecture guidance and maintenance. Ideal for continuous partnerships. |
| **Outcome-Based**    | Price tied to delivered results (e.g. per feature, per deployed agent). | E.g. fixed per launched AI agent or ROI-share. Aligns cost with business impact; good for clients wary of unknown costs. |

Each model trades off risk and flexibility. A fixed fee gives cost certainty (client likes it), while retainers ensure long-term alignment. The page should invite the prospect to discuss their needs, rather than locking into one style publicly.
