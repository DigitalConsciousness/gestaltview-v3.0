# Human Collaborator Brief

GestaltView is easiest to misunderstand if you look for the nearest familiar category.

It is not a productivity app, even though it creates outputs.
It is not a journal, even though it holds private reflection.
It is not a chatbot, even though Billy can converse.
It is not a dashboard, even though it visualizes data.

The simplest useful frame:

> GestaltView is a private cognitive home where fragments can land before they make sense, become visible, and later become meaning or artifacts with consent.

When collaborating:

- do not smooth out the weirdness,
- do not over-market it,
- distinguish source claim from implementation fact,
- preserve room distinctions,
- ask what each feature does to the user's safety and agency,
- keep Billy at the edge, not the center,
- make the next step real.
