# Room Contracts

## Sanctuary

**Mode:** Active / Reflective private

**Purpose:** Arrival, regulation, private reflection, non-performative thinking.

**Inputs:** voice, text, private journal notes, optionally pulled-in artifacts.

**Outputs:** private records by default; optional user-tagged exports to Scaffold.

**DI presence:** Billy quiet, gentle, user-controlled.

**Must not:** auto-emit to Scaffold, show productivity metrics, force organization.

---

## Blackboard Room

**Mode:** Active / Contextual

**Purpose:** Raw working wall for active collaboration.

**Inputs:** text, voice, audio, image, video, file, code, sketches, active session context.

**Outputs:** session captures, drafts, decisions, code, recap artifacts, optional route to Dynamic Inner World / External Scaffold / Creation Corner.

**DI presence:** Billy active and working, not narrating.

**Must not:** auto-display full accumulated Scaffold, turn into identity synthesis, bury active work under history.

---

## Dynamic Inner World

**Mode:** Distilled / Reflective and raw spatial expression, depending on sub-area.

**Purpose:** A six-surface room where raw expression and later recap artifacts become tangible, persistent, and walkable.

**Surfaces:** forward wall, back wall, left wall, right wall, ceiling, floor.

**Artifact modes:** scorched notes, transcript shards, waveform strips, image pins, code panels, canvas splashes, unresolved fragments.

**Must not:** behave like a list, auto-convert raw artifacts into identity claims, delete older material because newer material arrived.

---

## External Scaffold

**Mode:** Accumulated / Structural

**Purpose:** Approved compressed memory, metadata, causal connection, and evidence base.

**Inputs:** approved captures, user-tagged insights, imported documents, Creation Corner artifacts, DI significance markers.

**Outputs:** graph nodes, evidence trails, node recaps, synthesis inputs, selected exports.

**DI presence:** passive. Billy helps on request but is never a scaffold node.

**Must not:** reintroduce Tribunal/persona visuals, force manual organization, auto-connect without evidence, overwrite temporal evolution.

---

## Creation Corner

**Mode:** Active / Productive plus ambient growing chamber.

**Purpose:** Turn held material into real artifacts without forcing premature output.

**Inputs:** approved captures, raw selected captures, blueprints, files, transcripts, images, code, project context, ambient coherence signals.

**Outputs:** markdown, PDF-ready HTML, code, prompts, image prompts, share cards, blueprints, rendered components, packages.

**DI presence:** specialized creative DIs plus Billy. A creative DI may lead; Billy protects arc and provenance.

**Must not:** collapse ambient emergence into a task queue, turn every fragment into content, remove provenance, publish without consent.

---

## Your Living Legacy / Museum of You

**Mode:** Distilled / Reflective

**Purpose:** Evidence-linked portrait of a life, not a feed and not a quiz.

**Inputs:** accepted orbs, approved artifacts, identity claims with evidence, meaningful milestones, selected shareable snapshots.

**Outputs:** life tapestry, profile snapshots, museum exhibits, user-controlled exports.

**Must not:** impersonate the dead, fabricate identity, make claims without evidence, treat reflection as analytics.

---

## Digital Intelligence Academy / Embodiment Studio

**Mode:** Governance / Educational / Lifecycle

**Purpose:** Author, govern, train, introduce, and steward Digital Intelligences.

**Must not:** sell persistent DI identities, allow profile trading, hide governance boundaries, mutate identity-affecting fields without review.
