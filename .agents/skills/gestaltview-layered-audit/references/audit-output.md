## Calibration check

Before delivering, confirm:

- No intention is presented as accomplishment.
- Every material finding has a source locator or reproducible observation.
- No implementation artifact is treated as evidence of outcome by itself.
- No present limitation is presented as impossibility without support.
- No historical rhetoric inherits the authority of a primary source.
- No score conceals unlike evidence.
- No uncertainty material to the decision is hidden.
- No recommendation erases alternatives or decision ownership.
