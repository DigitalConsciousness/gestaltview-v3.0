# Evidence and developmental states

Use these labels to prevent intention, rhetoric, implementation, and proof from collapsing into one another. Do not convert them into numeric scores.

## Evidence classifications

| Label | Meaning | Suitable language |
|---|---|---|
| Source record | Contemporaneous words, files, events, or raw observations | “The source records…” |
| Demonstrated | Directly observed behavior, persisted state, test result, or reproducible execution | “The current surface demonstrates…” |
| Corroborated | Supported by more than one meaningfully independent source or method | “Multiple independent sources support…” |
| External support | Credible outside research, precedent, standard, or comparison | “External evidence supports…” |
| Interpretation | A reasoned reading of available evidence | “This suggests…” |
| Self-report | A participant’s account of experience or intention | “Keith reports…” / “The collaborator states…” |
| Aspiration | A desired future state, commitment, or design direction | “The system intends…” |
| Rhetoric | Persuasive or significance-bearing language not independently established | “The document characterizes…” |
| Unknown | Evidence is missing, inaccessible, conflicting, or insufficient | “The available evidence does not determine…” |

An AI-generated statement is not independent corroboration merely because several models repeated it. Shared source material, training influence, or framing can produce correlated agreement.

## Developmental states

Use the narrowest state the evidence supports:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Emerging | A pattern or need is visible but not yet stably named |
| Articulated | The idea or intended behavior is explicitly described |
| Modeled | A contract, schema, design, or architecture represents it |
| Prototyped | A limited or simulated implementation can be interacted with |
| Integrated | It connects to adjacent real system surfaces |
| Operational | It executes through the intended boundary and leaves a usable result |
| Evaluated | Its outcomes have been tested against explicit criteria |
| Maintained | It has current ownership, monitoring, repair, and documentation |

States are not a mandatory ladder. A surface can be operational but poorly articulated, or modeled faithfully while not yet integrated. Record mixed states by thread instead of averaging them.

## Enforcement classifications

Separate promises by the mechanism that gives them force:

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Moral commitment | A declared value or obligation |
| Social or operator policy | A rule enforced by human practice or organizational procedure |
| Model-facing instruction | Context or prompt intended to influence model behavior |
| Deterministic gate | Code, permissions, schema constraints, or infrastructure that prevents or requires an action |
| Evaluated behavior | A behavior repeatedly tested under defined conditions |
| Aspirational position | A direction not yet operationally guaranteed |

Avoid “cannot,” “always,” “never,” “hardcoded,” or “guaranteed” unless a deterministic mechanism and its relevant boundary are shown.

## Epistemic checks

For every material conclusion, ask:

1. What exact evidence changes the conclusion?
2. Is the evidence about intention, existence, operation, outcome, or external validity?
3. Is the source independent, or did it inherit the same framing?
4. Could a simpler explanation fit the observation?
5. What would falsify or revise the interpretation?
6. Is uncertainty visible where the decision maker will see it?
7. Does rhetoric receive more retrieval weight than original observation?

Preserve contradictory evidence. Do not force resolution merely to produce a clean narrative.
