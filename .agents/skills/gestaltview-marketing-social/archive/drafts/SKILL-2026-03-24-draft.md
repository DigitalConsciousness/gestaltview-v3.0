# Marketing & Social

Last reviewed: 2026-03-24

Use this skill when GestaltView needs to speak clearly in public.

## Workflow

1. Read `references/messaging-lanes.md` first.
2. Read `references/social-playbook.md` for channel execution.
3. Classify every major claim into documented / needs translation / design target.
4. Write toward clarity, proof, and specificity.
5. Only widen into visionary language after the practical frame is established.

## Core rules

- Show the evidence chain before the grand claim.
- Translate, do not deflate.
- Do not use cold-audience copy that overreaches clinical or scientific validation.
- Public writing should lead with the human problem and the working system.
- Social content should isolate one strong proof point per post whenever possible.
