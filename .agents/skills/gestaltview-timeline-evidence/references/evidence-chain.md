## Framing rules

- Use “documented,” “timestamped,” “anchored,” and “auditable” precisely.
- If you do not have the artifact itself in hand, refer to the repo’s description of the artifact rather than overstating certainty.
- Separate third-party recognition from self-authored interpretation.
