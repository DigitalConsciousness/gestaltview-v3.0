# Credibility Guardrails

## Use the lane model
- **Lane 1**: documented and citable.
- **Lane 2**: real but needs translation for a cold audience.
- **Lane 3**: design target or forward-looking plan.

## Translate, do not shrink
- Replace vague grandeur with specific buyer value and proof.
- Keep the architecture ambitious, but package it in legible business language.

## Good business-language bridges
- “proof layer” instead of “magic”
- “consulting practice and product ecosystem” instead of “everything app”
- “pilot / partnership / licensing pathway” instead of unsupported market dominance claims
