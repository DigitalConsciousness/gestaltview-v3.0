# Buyer Personas and Offers

## Primary buyer groups
- **Founders / teams** needing custom AI collaborator systems.
- **Neurodivergent users / creators** needing cognition-respecting tools.
- **Career-transition users** for Resume Rockstar-style services.
- **Institutions / care-adjacent partners** for Alzheimer's, recovery, or identity-preservation pilots.
- **Investors / accelerators / strategic partners** evaluating the platform and moat.

## Offer ladder
1. **Free/public proof** — site, demos, public artifacts.
2. **Diagnostic / strategy engagement** — architecture review, product framing, AI workflow design.
3. **Custom implementation** — bespoke AI system or product prototype.
4. **Recurring product access** — subscriptions, memberships, or premium access.
5. **Institutional partnership / license** — larger scoped engagements.

## Offer-writing pattern
For each offer define:
- buyer,
- pain,
- product / service,
- proof,
- next step,
- what is live now vs planned.
