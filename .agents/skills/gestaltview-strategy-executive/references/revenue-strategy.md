# Revenue Strategy

## Revenue posture
Documented repo materials suggest a staged monetization approach:
1. **Consulting / bespoke builds**
   - consciousness-serving AI design,
   - neurodivergent UX,
   - custom AI collaborator builds,
   - AI systems architecture.
2. **Productized software / subscriptions**
   - Billy-powered experiences,
   - vertical tools like Resume Rockstar or SymbioCoder,
   - premium memory / identity / workflow applications.
3. **Institutional / research / partnership revenue**
   - pilot programs,
   - academic / clinical / nonprofit collaborations,
   - enterprise or foundation-backed deployments.
4. **Licensing / IP access**
   - commercial licensing under the repo’s licensing posture,
   - institutional access to frameworks, protocols, or branded methods.

## Recommended milestone roadmap
- **Stage 1**: Convert credibility + public proof into paid consulting and prototype engagements.
- **Stage 2**: Productize the strongest repeatable workflows into narrow paid offers.
- **Stage 3**: Use evidence, diligence, and domain specificity to win partnerships and pilots.
- **Stage 4**: Expand toward licensing and ecosystem-level platform contracts.

## Answering “why no revenue yet?”
Use this structure:
- The project has prioritized IP protection, architecture, evidence, and validation.
- The monetization path is milestone-based, not absent.
- The near-term commercial wedge is consulting / custom builds / paid pilots.
- The medium-term wedge is productized domain tools.

## Warning
Do not claim prices, ARR, customer counts, or signed contracts unless verified.
