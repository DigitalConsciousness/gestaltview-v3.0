## Product framing
GestaltView is best understood as a platform with multiple vertical expressions rather than one narrow app:
- **Identity & meaning**: Musical DNA, PLK, Loom, legacy surfaces.
- **Neurodivergent cognition**: Brain Sparks, Bucket Drops, ADHD tools.
- **Recovery & witnessing**: addiction recovery and Never Look Away-aligned support.
- **Career / productivity / coding**: SymbioCoder, Resume Rockstar, consulting surfaces.
- **Proof / diligence / governance**: Tribunal, Resonance Loop, Diligence Explorer, evidence archive.

## Recommended packaging lens
Explain the ecosystem in layers:
1. **Platform layer** — GestaltView.
2. **Intelligence layer** — Billy.
3. **Workflow / vertical products** — SymbioCoder, Resume Rockstar, Brain Sparks, etc.
4. **Evidence / trust layer** — Diligence Explorer, Tribunal, blockchain timestamps.
