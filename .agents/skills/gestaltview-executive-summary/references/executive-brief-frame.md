# Executive Brief Frame

## Core formulation

GestaltView is a consciousness-serving AI platform designed to preserve human complexity instead of flattening it. It combines identity-preserving language capture (PLK), low-friction thought capture (Bucket Drops), contextual synthesis (Loom / Context Weaver), and a live intelligence layer (Billy) across multiple domain experiences.

## What to include

- solo/unfunded origin and founder context
- live TypeScript/Vite runtime plus serverless/API and Supabase knowledge layer
- neurodivergent-first design as architecture, not cosmetic positioning
- public proof and documented evidence chain
- explicit statement of what is not yet clinically or academically validated

## Good closing asks

- partnership
- funding for build/validation
- research collaboration
- pilot deployment
- enterprise white-label conversation
