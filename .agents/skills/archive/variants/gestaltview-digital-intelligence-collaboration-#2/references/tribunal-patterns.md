# Tribunal Patterns

## Tribunal framing
Use the Tribunal as a governance and convergence pattern: multiple models or perspectives independently pressure-test the same material and surface recurring structure.

## Good uses
- adversarial critique,
- consensus-checking,
- perspective diversification,
- evidence-led synthesis.

## Avoid
- presenting it as clinical validation,
- claiming scientific proof where only structured convergence is documented,
- using “unprecedented” as lazy shorthand without evidence.
