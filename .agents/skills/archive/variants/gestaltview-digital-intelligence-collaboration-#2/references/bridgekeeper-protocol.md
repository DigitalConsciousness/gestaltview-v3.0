# Bridgekeeper Protocol

## Human role
- transmit context,
- preserve PLK and exact language,
- identify what is sacred / non-negotiable,
- decide when outputs are aligned,
- prevent agents from flattening the work into generic artifacts.

## AI role split
- **Designer**: structure and propose.
- **Executor**: implement and expand.
- **Validator**: critique, check, and pressure-test.

## Important safeguard
Do not attribute autonomous consensus or independence beyond what was actually documented. Describe the workflow accurately and keep the evidence trail intact.
