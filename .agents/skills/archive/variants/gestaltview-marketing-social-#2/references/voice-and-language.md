# Voice and Language

## Core posture
- Accurate translation, not deflation.
- Evidence-forward, not hype-forward.
- Human, witnessed, intimate, and technically literate.
- Distinguish clearly between documented proof and design targets.

## Preferred themes
- infrastructure for being seen,
- scars become code,
- neurodivergent-first systems,
- AI as witness / collaborator rather than extractor,
- evidence-backed emergence,
- products born from lived experience.

## Cold-audience guideline
On first reference, ground GestaltView-specific terms in plain English. Use the lane system to prevent overclaiming.
