## Suggested channel roles
- **Website / GitHub**: proof, architecture, demos, diligence.
- **Medium**: deep philosophy, origin story, founder essays.
- **LinkedIn**: credibility, executive framing, consulting offers, milestones.
- **X / Threads-like short form**: sharp ideas, build-in-public fragments, quotable observations.
- **TikTok / Instagram / YouTube**: founder story, walkthroughs, emotional/product narratives, demo clips.
- **Discord / Reddit**: community conversation, beta users, nuanced discussion.

## Messaging cadence model
- **Proof posts**: what shipped, what changed, what evidence exists.
- **Founder insight posts**: lived-experience-to-architecture translation.
- **Product posts**: one use case, one demo, one route.
- **Invitation posts**: collaboration, consulting, pilot, advisor, or partner asks.
