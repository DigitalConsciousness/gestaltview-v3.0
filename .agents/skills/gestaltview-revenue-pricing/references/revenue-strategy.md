# Revenue Strategy Heuristics

## Offer ladder framing

- **Core** = accessible identity-preserving daily companion
- **Pro** = full-stack cognitive operating system for solo founders / creators / builders
- **Enterprise** = organizational Billy + white-label consciousness-serving infrastructure

## Useful future revenue tracks

- vertical-specific domain lanes (recovery, Alzheimer’s legacy, portfolio/career, creator workflows)
- higher-trust diligence/evidence packages
- white-label Billy deployments
- implementation/consulting attached to the live portfolio
- cross-repo product bundles once sibling repos are aligned

## Guardrails

- Never promise clinical outcomes or unsupported efficacy.
- Make clear which features are live, demo-mode, or roadmap.
- Keep pricing explanations legible to a skeptical user who has been burned by overhyped AI tools.
