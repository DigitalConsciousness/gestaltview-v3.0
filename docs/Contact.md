## 🧠 Founder

*Keith Soyka is the founder and architect of **GestaltView**, a neurodivergent‑first AI platform for self‑discovery, cognitive justice, and AI–human collaboration, built solo and unfunded since May 2025. 
He brings 22+ years of operations and team leadership, and treats his own “exploded picture mind” as the first R&D lab for the system—turning lived adversity (ADHD, addiction recovery, long‑term closeting, myocarditis) into concrete product features like the Loom Approach, Bucket Drops, and the Personal Language Key.*
