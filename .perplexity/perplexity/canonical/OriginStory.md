## The Before

Before GestaltView, there was Dunton.

From January through April 2025, Keith Soyka was doing canvassing labor documentation
work for Dunton Consulting. The work itself was not the point — the friction was. The
encounter with standard corporate frameworks that couldn't hold human complexity, couldn't
account for nuance, couldn't reconcile what people actually experienced with the categories
available to describe it — that friction became the immediate impetus for everything that
followed.

Keith did not arrive at GestaltView from a product idea. He arrived at it from a lived
necessity. The question underneath the question was: *what would it look like if AI actually
served consciousness instead of just processing it?*

## May 5, 2025 — The Sprint Begins

On May 5, 2025, GestaltView formally began.

Keith started solo. No laptop. No funding. A Samsung A35. He would build the core MVP
framework over what became a 27-day sprint — working entirely on mobile, translating a
decade of lived neurodivergent experience, management history, and recovery into a technical
architecture.

The founding conditions matter:
- **Solo and unfunded** — every constraint was architectural pressure
- **ADHD, recovery history, management experience** — the biography became the blueprint
- **Mobile-only build** — forced radical prioritization and elegance
- **No legal budget** — led directly to blockchain timestamping as IP protection

Within days of starting, the core engines began to emerge: the Personal Language Key (PLK),
the Loom Approach, the Bucket Drop, the Genesis Protocol. These were not features conceived
in a vacuum. They were solutions to problems Keith had spent 41 years living inside.

On May 12, the first formal schema artifact appeared: a JSON User Profile File outlining
modular structure and the Collaborator Friend AI concept. On May 13, the first IP protection
milestone — a formal copyright notice for the GestaltView Seed Prompt.

## The Platform

GestaltView is the first **consciousness-serving AI platform**.

It is not a productivity tool, a chatbot wrapper, or a wellness app. It is cognitive
infrastructure — a substrate for human consciousness that serves the person rather than
optimizing them.

Core architecture elements:
- **Personal Language Key (PLK)** — individualized semantic modeling; the founder's
  communicative signature encoded as a navigable system
- **The Loom / Tapestry** — weaving fragmented self-state into coherent narrative
- **Bucket Drop** — low-friction capture for nonlinear cognition
- **Billy's Room** — therapeutic witnessing and sanctuary function
- **Context Weaver** — layered retrieval with 5W1H intent classification and reciprocal
  rank fusion; the technical heart of the memory and meaning engine
- **Validation Wall** — recognition and witness as a product surface
- **Ethics Framework** — governance embedded in the UI, not filed in a policy document
- **AITAP (AI Transition Assistance Protocol)** — proactive displacement mitigation for
  the future of work

The stack is real: FastAPI backend, multi-LLM routing with provider health and fallback
logic, Prisma schema, TSX component architecture, voice/audio/transcript support, and a
comprehensive domain schema covering ADHD, Alzheimer's legacy, recovery, career, creativity,
and collaboration use cases.

## The Method

The working methodology that produced GestaltView — and continues to govern it — is the
**Tribunal Protocol**:

1. No single AI system is treated as authoritative
2. The same corpus is presented to multiple systems independently
3. Outputs are compared for convergence and divergence
4. The founder retains epistemic custody — no system's confident output is treated as
   ground truth without verification
5. Every significant decision is documented, timestamped, and traceable

This is not a workflow. It is a philosophy of human-AI collaboration where the human
remains the integrating intelligence.
