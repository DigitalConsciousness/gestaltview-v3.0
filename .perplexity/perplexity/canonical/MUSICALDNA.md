## The Somatic Modes

**Bi-Lateral (Bilateral):** Left ↔ Right hemisphere integration. EMDR-adjacent processing.
The processing mode. For integration, understanding, working through.

**Tri-Lateral (Trilateral):** Three-point upward spiral. Full skull immersion.
The immersion mode. For feeling held in three-dimensional space.

**Quad-Lateral (Quadlateral):** Figure-8 path. Speed, fog, and color sync with the music.
The regulation mode. For when you're at your edge and need steadying before you can go deeper.

## The DNA Vectors

Each song carries a DNA vector [introspection, intensity, connection, catharsis] on a 0–1 scale.
These vectors are used to match songs to nervous system states and therapeutic needs.

High introspection + high catharsis = processing mode (Bilateral recommended)
High connection + high intensity = immersion mode (Trilateral recommended)
High catharsis + low connection = regulation mode (Quadlateral first, always)
