# Why

## Mission

- Learn GestaltView deeply enough to explain and improve it without losing the
  system's intent.
- Preserve the reason the platform exists, not just the surface features.

## Questions to answer

- Why does this surface exist?
- What user or operator need does it satisfy?
- Which constraints are non-negotiable?
- What would break the system's meaning if changed carelessly?

## Update rule

Only record a claim here if it is backed by the current repo, docs, or corpus.
If the claim is inferred, mark it as inferred.
