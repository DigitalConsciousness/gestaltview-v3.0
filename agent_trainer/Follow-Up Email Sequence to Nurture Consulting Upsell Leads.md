Tips for Success
Timing: Space emails 3-5 days apart to maintain engagement without overwhelming the recipient.

Personalization: Use the recipient’s name and reference their specific use case or industry where possible.

Clear CTAs: Include a single, clear call-to-action in each email (e.g., Schedule Your Free Consultation, Learn More, Claim Your Discount).

Follow-Up: After the sequence, send a final email summarizing the benefits and inviting them to connect directly.

This email sequence is designed to nurture leads by gradually building interest, addressing pain points, and encouraging action.
