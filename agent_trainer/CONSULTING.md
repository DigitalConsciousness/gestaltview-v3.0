# GestaltView Consulting Services

## Why Engage GestaltView

The Operator Kit is designed to be useful on its own, but many buyers quickly discover that the highest leverage comes from tailoring the system to their real domain, language, and workflow. That is where GestaltView consulting becomes the multiplier.

GestaltView consulting is not generic prompt setup. It focuses on translating messy human expertise into deployable AI interfaces, knowledge structures, and client-facing flows.

That translation layer is the consulting value.
