# Pricing Tiers

| Tier | Seats | Fragments | Memory | Queries / month | Price |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Solo Spark | 1 | 100 | 50 | 500 | $49 one-time |
| Studio | 5 | 1,000 | 500 | 5,000 | $149/mo or $999/yr |
| Growth | 25 | 10,000 | 5,000 | 50,000 | $449/mo or $3,499/yr |
| Enterprise | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Custom |

## Discount Layer

- Bootstrap discount: eligible solo operators can receive a standing discount on Studio.
- Founding member pricing: limited early-buyer discount for initial customers.
- Annual billing: discounted effective monthly rate.

## Positioning

- Solo Spark validates the concept.
- Studio is the default consulting and small-team tier.
- Growth supports broader team use and analytics.
- Enterprise adds white-label and support depth.
